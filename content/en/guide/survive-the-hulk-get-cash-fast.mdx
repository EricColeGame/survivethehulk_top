## Turn Round Survival Into Steady Payouts

If daily rewards are the faucet, round survival is the bucket. Reaching the end of the match timer is the core survivor objective, and longer survival means better progression and better payouts. Dying in the first thirty seconds pays almost nothing.

Use these tactics to stretch every round:

- **Hide inside lockers.** Lockers are scattered along facility hallways and function as dedicated hiding containers. Stepping inside breaks line of sight entirely, which is often enough to survive a Hulk sweep.
- **Crawl and slide through shortcuts.** Survivors can crawl through low openings and vents, and break glass to create new paths. These routes are usually too tight for the Hulk to follow directly.
- **Conserve stamina.** Sprinting everywhere drains your bar fast. If you are exhausted when the Hulk rounds the corner, you cannot sprint or slide away — you are simply caught.
- **Read the audio cues.** Punch impacts and collapsing debris tell you the Hulk is working through an adjacent room. That noise is a free warning to relocate before he breaks through your wall.

| Survivor Tactic | What It Buys You | Cash Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Locker hiding | Full line-of-sight break | Extends round time |
| Vent and crawl routes | Paths the Hulk cannot easily follow | Extends round time |
| Stamina management | Escape options when it matters | Prevents early death |
| Audio tracking | Advance warning of the Hulk's position | Lets you reposition early |
| Objective play near the exit | Faster clean finishes | Consistent round completion |

One habit separates high earners from everyone else: play the round out. Even if you spend the last ninety seconds crouched behind a low structure, the timer still counts. Boring rounds pay the same as dramatic ones.
