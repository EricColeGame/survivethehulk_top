### Why Low-Clearance Crawlspaces Dominate

Ventilation shafts and crawlspaces sit at the top of the list for one simple reason: the Hulk's collision box is enormous. A model that wide cannot slide into a narrow duct without awkward repositioning, which gives you a free head start the moment the beast commits to a chase.

The real value, though, is the two-way exit. While you crawl, keep an eye on both openings. If the hunter plants themselves at one end and winds up a smash, crawl out the opposite side and break for the main hall instead of waiting to be crushed.

### Dual-Exit Offices and the Art of the Loop

Mid-level offices offer natural line-of-sight blockers: desks, cubicle partitions, and filing cabinets. Crouching behind solid furniture hides your model from a passing patrol, and the clutter slows a hunter who tries to cut through.

The bigger advantage is routing. If the door shatters, you can vault a broken window or side door, loop back around the cubicles, and slip behind the brute before they reorient. Two exits turn a dead end into a trap for the monster.

## Lockers vs. Crawlspaces vs. Crouching: Which Hide Type Should You Use?

Environmental interaction is the core of survivor defense, and the most common mistake is sprinting straight to the nearest metal locker the second the siren sounds. Lockers work — but only in specific situations.

| Hide Type | Concealment Level | Stamina Cost | Exit Speed | Best Use Case |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Locker units | 100% (fully hidden) | None | Slow (animation delay) | Short cover during a distant patrol |
| Crawlspace tubes | 80% (partial view) | Low | Moderate | Chokepoint stalling and directional jukes |
| Low crouch behind props | 70% (model hidden) | Minimal | Instant (sprint ready) | Breaking line of sight mid-chase |
| Shadow alcoves | 50% (lighting dependent) | None | Instant | Emergency stamina recharge |

A locker removes your model from direct sight, but it is a coffin if the hunter watches you climb in. Community reports consistently flag locker banks as the single most common place survivors get eliminated, because a hunter who sees you enter simply waits out the animation and swings.

Three habits keep lockers useful rather than fatal:

- **Toggle crouch constantly.** A lower stance keeps your head and nameplate below window frames and partition tops.
- **Never crowd a locker bank.** Two or three survivors in one cluster invites an area-of-effect smash that catches everyone.
- **Check your exit first.** Do not enter a hideaway if debris or a fallen wall prop blocks the door in front of it.

Move away from fully enclosed rooms. A sealed room with a destroyed door gives you nothing against a fast Locate ping. Instead, drift toward open areas with vertical obstacles — Storage Bay catwalks, courtyard perimeters, container stacks — and kite the beast in wide circles until the victory screen appears.

## Movement, Stamina, and the Shadow Follow Technique

A stationary survivor is an easy target. Strong play follows a loop: enter a hiding spot, watch the perimeter, listen for destruction, and rotate before the hunter sweeps your sector. Stamina is the engine that keeps that loop running.

Rotating between secondary locations keeps your chosen **Survive The Hulk hiding spots** viable even after the map starts crumbling. If you burn your entire stamina bar sprinting into a dead end, no nook will save you.

- **Never sprint in safety.** Walk or crouch through quiet corridors so the meter refills completely.
- **Skip pointless jumps.** Jumping drains stamina fast and does little against wide-sweeping punches.
- **Hug corners when you rotate.** Cutting tight turns keeps you out of sightlines longer than running down the middle of a hall.

One technique that shows up repeatedly in player experience discussions is the "shadow follow." Because the hunter's field of view is focused forward on breaking doors and checking lockers, trailing behind their patrol path keeps you in territory they have already cleared. Keep a gap of at least two room lengths and always stay within reach of a pillar or side door in case the hunter spins around.

## FAQ: Survive The Hulk Hiding Spots

### What are the best Survive The Hulk hiding spots for beginners?

Low-clearance ventilation ducts and double-door administrative offices are the safest starting points. Both give you real visual cover and an immediate exit if the monster breaks through the perimeter wall.

### Can the Hulk see me inside a locker?

Not directly — climbing into a locker removes your model from line of sight. But if the hunter triggers a Locate scan, your position is still highlighted, and an experienced player will simply smash the locker open. Treat lockers as short-term staging points, not permanent shelter.

### How do I escape after the monster finds my hiding spot?

Slide under the opening punch or sprint past the hunter's non-dominant side while the smash animation is still recovering. Break line of sight with a corner or a low prop, then move to a new hiding area instead of running in a straight line.

### Why do experienced players avoid bathrooms and dead ends?

Single-entry rooms leave zero escape vectors. Once the monster fills the doorway, their wide hitbox and sweeping attacks make escape impossible regardless of your remaining health. Always prioritize a hiding spot with two ways out.
